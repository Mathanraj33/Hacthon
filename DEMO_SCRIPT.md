Demonstrate how our platform helps city administrators assess urban sustainability, identify problem areas, and make data-driven decisions using AI.

Presenter: Member 4

# Introduction (30 seconds)

Good morning/afternoon judges.

We are Team <Team Name>, and today we are presenting our **Intelligent Urban Sustainability Assessment System**.

Our solution combines environmental, infrastructure, and urban data into a single sustainability assessment platform that helps governments monitor city performance and make informed planning decisions.

---

# Problem Statement (45 seconds)

Modern cities collect data from many different sources, but this information is often scattered across multiple systems.

As a result:
- It is difficult to understand the overall sustainability of a city.
- Decision-makers lack a unified sustainability score.
- Policy evaluation is slow and often relies on manual analysis.

Our project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, AI-assisted sustainability assessment platform.

---

# Solution Overview (45 seconds)

Our system collects data from multiple public sources, processes it, and calculates sustainability scores across key urban indicators.

Users can:
- View sustainability scores on an interactive dashboard.
- Explore ward-level performance on a map.
- Understand why a score was assigned.
- Receive AI-generated recommendations.
- Simulate policy changes to compare outcomes.

---

# Live Demo (3 minutes)

### Step 1 – Dashboard

We begin on the main dashboard, where users can view the overall sustainability score along with key performance indicators.

---

### Step 2 – Map

Next, we open the interactive city map.

Each ward is color-coded based on its sustainability score, making it easy to identify high-performing and low-performing areas.

---

### Step 3 – Score Explanation

Selecting a ward displays:
- Overall score
- Pillar-wise scores
- Indicator contributions

This helps users understand the reasoning behind the assessment.

---

### Step 4 – Recommendations

The platform generates practical recommendations to improve sustainability.

For example:
- Increase green cover
- Improve waste management
- Enhance public transport

Each recommendation is linked to the indicators affecting the score.

---

### Step 5 – Policy Simulator

We now simulate a policy change.

For example, increasing green space or improving public transport.

The system recalculates the sustainability score in real time, helping decision-makers compare different scenarios before implementation.

---

### Step 6 – Report Generation

Finally, users can download a sustainability report containing:
- Current scores
- Visualizations
- Recommendations
- Summary insights

This report can support planning discussions and decision-making.
